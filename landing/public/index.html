How it works

From a photo to a fixed problem in four steps

You always know where your report is and what happens next.

  1. 1

    Report

    Send a photo, the location and a short description from your phone.

  2. 2

    Review

    The barangay office checks your report and accepts it, or tells you why it cannot be accepted.

  3. 3

    Assign

    Valid reports are sent to the right team.

  4. 4

    Resolve

    The team updates the progress until it is done, and you are notified at every step.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Do I need an account to report a problem?

Yes. You register once with an email and password, so your reports can be followed up and the status can be sent back to you.

Who can see my report?

Only you, the barangay office, and the team that is assigned to fix it.

How long until my report is fixed?

It depends on the problem. You can follow every step live, and you are notified at each change.

Is there a mobile app?

The Android app is being built. Until then you can use Local Community Services from your phone's browser.

See something that needs fixing?

Send a report in under a minute and watch it move from Pending to Resolved.
