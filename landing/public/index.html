How it works

From a photo to a fixed problem in four steps

You always know where your report is and what happens next.

  1. 1

    Report

    Send a photo, the location and a short description from your phone.

  2. 2

    Review

    The barangay office checks your report and accepts it, or tells you why it cannot be accepted.

  3. 3

    Assign

    Valid reports are sent to the right team.

  4. 4

    Resolve

    The team updates the progress until it is done, and you are notified at every step.

What can you report?

Any problem in your neighbourhood

Pick the type of problem when you send a report.

Streetlight

A broken, flickering or dark street lamp.

Garbage

Uncollected garbage or an illegal dumping spot.

Road & potholes

Potholes, cracks and damaged roads.

Flooding & drainage

Clogged canals and flooded streets.

Noise

Loud noise that disturbs the neighbours.

Others

Anything else, such as water supply or stray animals.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Is it free?

Yes. Creating an account and sending reports costs nothing.

Do I need an account to report a problem?

Yes. You register once with an email and password, and confirm your email, so your reports can be followed up and the status can be sent back to you.

Who can see my report?

Only you, the barangay office, and the team that is assigned to fix it. Other residents cannot see it.

How long until my report is fixed?

It depends on the problem and on the team's workload. You can follow every step live, and you are notified at each change.

What if I have no internet?

Sending a report needs a connection, because the photo and location are uploaded. Try again when you are back online, or visit the barangay hall. For emergencies, call the hotlines at the bottom of this page.

Is there a mobile app?

An Android app is being built. Until then you can use Local Community Services from your phone's browser.

Is this for emergencies?

No. For fires, crimes or medical emergencies call 911 or the hotlines below. This site is for everyday community problems.

See something that needs fixing?

Send a report in under a minute and watch it move from Pending to Resolved.
